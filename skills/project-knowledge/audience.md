# Audience Profile

## Primary Users

**Role:** General public — individuals seeking to use the product for personal purposes  
**Age Range:** 13–65  
**Tech Literacy:** Wide spectrum, from digital natives with high proficiency to users with minimal technology experience  
**Goals:** Complete tasks quickly and independently, without confusion or frustration

## Secondary Users

None defined. All users, regardless of age or ability, are treated as primary users and must receive equal consideration in design and content decisions.

## User Context

**Where:** Likely used across devices — mobile, tablet, and desktop — in a variety of environments including home, school, and public spaces  
**When:** On demand, as needed — not necessarily in a focused or distraction-free setting  
**How:** Self-directed navigation; users are not guided by a human intermediary and must be able to orient themselves without prior training or instruction

## Complexity Level

**Low.** Content and navigation must be simple enough for a first-time user with limited literacy or tech confidence to succeed on their first attempt. No feature, label, or instruction should require prior knowledge to understand.

## Accessibility Requirements

- Navigation must be immediately intuitive — no learning curve
- Critical actions must be reachable in as few steps as possible
- Labels, buttons, and instructions must use plain language
- Avoid jargon, acronyms, or assumed knowledge
- Visual hierarchy must guide the eye clearly to the next action
- Consider users who may struggle with reading — support with iconography, clear layout, and consistent patterns where possible

## Language & Tone Expectations

- **Plain language at all times** — aim for a Grade 6–8 reading level
- Short sentences, common words, active voice
- Warm and approachable, never clinical or technical
- Instructional content must be direct: tell users exactly what to do, not what the system does
- Avoid walls of text — break information into digestible chunks
- Consistency in terminology is critical; never use two different words for the same thing

## What Users Care About Most

- **Speed** — getting to what they need without detours
- **Simplicity** — not feeling overwhelmed or lost
- **Clarity** — understanding what to do at every step without having to think too hard
- **Confidence** — feeling capable and in control, not confused or excluded
- **Trust** — a product that feels reliable, not confusing or unpredictable
